### Upper Push — FULL template, RPE 7-8 (back-to-back with run today)

**Sequencing today:** Run → 5 min cool-down walk → quick refuel (banana + whey shake, ~15-20 min while it hits) → lift warm-up → working sets. Total session start-to-finish ~2 hours.

**Why this works for this specific pairing:**
- The run loads legs; Push loads chest/shoulders/triceps → minimal direct muscle interference.
- Easy Z2 doesn't deplete CNS the way a quality session would.
- The big risk on back-to-back doubles is glycogen + low blood sugar tanking the lift. The mid-session refuel solves that.

**Mid-session fuel (non-optional today):** 1 banana + 25-35 g whey + 250-400 mL water immediately after the cool-down walk. Sit, let it land for 15-20 min. If you skip this, the lift goes to garbage and you'll know it on set 2 of bench.

**Load discipline:** RPE 7-8 ceiling on working sets — the cut-phase standard. RPE 8 = "could do 2 more reps with good form." Stop there. Not RPE 9 (1 rep in reserve) and not RPE 10 (failure). Today is the first logged session in 14 days *and* it follows a run — the goal is a clean session at the protocol ceiling, not a hero session that tanks Thursday's Pull. If you find yourself at RPE 8 on the second-to-last rep instead of the last, the run took its tax — accept it, log it, and move on.

**Loads:** work back toward last logged. Bench last logged at 40 kg × 10/10/8/7 (Feb 18). Open at 37.5-40 kg and let the reps tell you. If 40 kg moves at RPE 7 on set 1, hold it. If it grinds past RPE 8, drop 2.5 kg for the next set. Same logic for OHP (last 30 kg × 10/8/10).

**Set/rep targets** (full template, per `protocols/training.md`):

| Slot | Exercise                       | Sets x Reps   | Rest        | Notes                                                  |
| ---- | ------------------------------ | ------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Bench press                    | 4 x 8-12      | 2-3 min     | Open 37.5-40 kg. Hit RPE 7-8 on set 1, hold load if reps clean |
| 2    | Overhead press                 | 3 x 8-12      | 2-3 min     | Open 27.5-30 kg. Strict, no leg drive                  |
| 3    | DB chest fly or cable cross    | 3 x 10-15     | 90 sec      | Stretch + squeeze. RPE 8 on the last set               |

| 5    | Lateral raises                 | 3 x 12-15     | 60-75 sec   | Pause at top, controlled negative                       |

| 6b   | Pallof press or cable woodchop | 3 x 10/side   | 45-60 sec   | Anti-rotation                                           |

**Rest discipline:** compound presses (slots 1-2) get the long rest because force output and RPE accuracy depend on full ATP/PCr recovery — rushing these sandbags the working weight you'd otherwise hit. Accessories and isolation (slots 3-5) live in the metabolic-stress lane where 60-90 sec is the sweet spot. Don't get cute and short-rest the bench to "save time" — you'll just log a worse session.

Rotation note: this is the template default. Swap exercise 3 or 4 in the next Push session for the rotation rule.

**Log every working weight.** No load logged → no progression decision on the next session, and that is the exact loop we are exiting. Weight + reps per top set in the daily file, minimum. Bonus: log RPE per exercise — that's what tells us next week whether to add load or hold.

## Nutrition (~2,400 cal — Re-entry Easy/Strength tier, training day breakfast-in)

Back-to-back session collapses the AM and PM fuel windows into one block. The carb-partitioning rule (40 % between run and lift, 20 % pre-lift) doesn't apply the same way today — instead, the mid-session refuel does the work, and a real meal lands ~60-90 min after the lift.

| Meal               | Time                                  | Target                                                          | Notes |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Pre-run (optional) | 20-30 min before run                  | 1 banana only if legs heavy or hungry. Otherwise water + coffee | Don't force fuel — bricks in gut tank the run |
| **Mid-session refuel** | **immediately post-run, ~15-20 min before lift** | **1 banana + 25-35 g whey + creatine 5 g + 300-400 mL water, ~250 cal, 30 g P, 30 g C** | **Load-bearing today — do not skip** |
| Post-lift meal     | within 60 min of finishing            | ~700 cal, 50 g P, 90 g C, 18 g F (chicken/fish + big rice/potato + veg + olive oil) | Replaces both "breakfast" and "lunch" — combined refuel for the combined session |
| Snack              | mid-afternoon (~16:30-17:00)          | ~400 cal, 35 g P, 40 g C, 10 g F (Greek yogurt + fruit + nuts, or skyr + oats) | |
| Dinner             | 20:30-21:00                           | ~600 cal, 45 g P, 60 g C, 20 g F (lean protein + potato/pasta + veg) | ≥40 g pre-bed protein — non-negotiable on lift days |
| Pre-bed (optional) | if hungry                             | 200 g cottage cheese or skyr if dinner protein landed short      | Casein for overnight MPS |

**Targets:** ~2,400 cal | ≥160 g P (running ~180-190) | ~270 g C | ~75 g F

**Carb distribution check (back-to-back version):** mid-session refuel 30 g + post-session meal 90 g = 120 g (~45 %) inside the 90 min refuel window — covers glycogen replenishment for both run and lift. Snack + dinner top off the rest.
