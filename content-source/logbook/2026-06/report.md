**Steps (context only, not a lever):** weekly averages ran 9.1k → 12.5k → 13.7k → **8.8k** across the four weeks; the last week dropped with two desk-bound days. **Read:** encouraging, directionally correct, not yet proven. Hold the line — do **not** cut plan-day food, do **not** add hard running. ### Nutrition execution — the fat floor slipped three days running

A recurring wk4 flag worth fixing: the **60 g fat floor was missed Jun 23 (~52 g) and Jun 24 (~49 g)**, and only cleared Jun 25 (61 g) via a late almond add. The cause was consistent — lean protein swaps (lean fish, chicken) combined with omitted cooking oils (no lunch oil, no dinner avocado/oil). Protein and calories held every day; fat was the casualty. The fix (anchor fat early at the eggs breakfast plus a lunch oil, rather than chasing it at dinner) was applied Jun 24–26 and is the right standing rule.

This now matters more than it did: **isotretinoin absorption is fat-dependent.** With the microdose course started Jun 25, the iso dose should ride on the day's fattiest meal, and sub-floor fat days both blunt absorption and undercut the floor's hormonal rationale (already relevant on finasteride). Holding fat at ≥60 g is no longer just a macro target — take the iso with food that carries real fat.

Protein cleared the 165 g floor every logged day. Meal structure settled into the reinstated pattern — small breakfast + a guaranteed midafternoon protein feed (4 feeds easy/rest, 5 on quality/strength) — which the athlete reports blunts evening grazing. The recurring "Greek yogurt won't carry a protein bolus alone" issue showed up twice more (Jun 20, Jun 24): the protein-weak Mythos Ligeiro yogurt (~5 g/100 g) needs whey or skyr to hit a 30 g feed.
