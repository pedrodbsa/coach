**Execution notes:**
- **Indoor or outdoor?** Either works. If outdoor, HR targets shift to 148-158 (outdoor premium). Pacing targets stay the same.
- **Sub-T = "controlled discomfort."** You should be able to speak in short phrases. If you're gasping, you're too fast.
- **HR target is 145-152, not higher.** The Long variant sits lower on the sub-T spectrum — sustained, aerobic power work. Don't chase 155+.
- **Abort criteria:** If intervals require >165 bpm to hold pace, downgrade to easy run.
