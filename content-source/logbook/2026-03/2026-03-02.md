#### Training Partner Plan (Novice)

Same movement patterns, beginner-friendly. Goal: learn the movements, build confidence, leave feeling good (not destroyed). No DOMS death spiral.

| #  | Exercise              | Sets × Reps    | Weight / Cue          | Rest    | Notes                                       |
| -- | --------------------- | -------------- | --------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Goblet Squat          | 3 × 10-12     | 6-10 kg DB/KB         | 90 sec  | Hold weight at chest. Sit back, knees track toes. |
| 2  | Hip Thrust (machine or barbell) | 3 × 10-12 | Light (RPE 6-7) | 90 sec  | Pause 1s at top. Squeeze glutes hard.       |
| 3  | Walking Lunges        | 3 × 8/leg     | Bodyweight            | 90 sec  | Can do together — same exercise, you add DBs. |
| 4  | Leg Curl              | 3 × 12        | Light                 | 60 sec  | Can share the machine — alternate sets.     |
| 5  | Calf Raises           | 3 × 15        | Bodyweight or light   | 60 sec  | Can do together.                            |
| 6  | Plank                 | 3 × 20-30s    | Bodyweight            | 60 sec  | You do RKC plank, she does standard plank.  |

**Coaching cues for novice partner:**
- **Weight selection:** If the last 2 reps of a set aren't challenging, go heavier next set. If form breaks before rep 10, go lighter.
- **Tempo:** 2 seconds down, 1 second up. Control the weight — don't bounce or drop.
- **Breathing:** Exhale on the push/stand up (effort), inhale on the way down.
- **Rest:** Take the full rest. No rushing.
- **RPE target:** 6-7. Should feel like "I could do 3-4 more reps." This isn't about exhaustion — it's about learning.
- **Goblet squat over barbell squat:** Teaches proper squat depth and positioning without the complexity of a barbell. The weight acts as a counterbalance.
- **No deadlift for novice:** RDL requires hip hinge awareness that takes time to develop. Hip thrusts hit the same muscles with a simpler movement.
- **Total time:** ~35-40 min.

**Exercises you can do together:**
- Walking Lunges (you with DBs, she bodyweight — same floor space)
- Leg Curl (alternate sets on the machine)
- Calf Raises (side by side)
- Core at the end (you do RKC plank + hanging leg raise, she does standard plank)
