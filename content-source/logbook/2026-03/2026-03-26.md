## Plan

### Session: Easy 3km Shake-Out + 4 Strides (~20 min)

**Timing:** Late morning / lunchtime | **Distance:** ~3.5 km total | **Duration:** ~20-25 min
**Pre-run:** Water + coffee only (fasted — this is barely a run)

**Pre-run warm-up (Daily 5):**

1. Pogo Hops — 30s
2. A-Skips — 30s
3. Leg Swings (Front/Back) — 30s/side
4. Leg Swings (Lateral) — 30s/side
5. Walking Lunges — 30s

| Segment    | Distance    | Pace         | HR Target                               | Notes                                 |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Easy jog   | 2.5-3 km    | 6:30-7:30/km | <148 bpm (outdoor) / <142 (treadmill)  | RPE 3. Conversational. Barely effort. |
| Strides    | 4 x 20s     | Smooth accel to ~85-90% | N/A                          | Walk back 60s between. Relaxed, tall. |
| Cool-down  | 500m        | Walk         | <120 bpm                                | Walk it out. Done.                    |

**Execution notes:**

- **This is the last run before the race.** The purpose is neuromuscular activation — keep the legs from going stale during 2 days of complete rest (Fri-Sat).
- **Strides are NOT sprints.** Smooth acceleration to a brisk pace, hold 20 seconds, decelerate. Relaxed shoulders, high knees, quick feet. Think "race pace turnover" not "all-out."
- **If legs feel springy:** Great. That's the taper. Enjoy it. Don't extend the run.
- **If legs feel flat/heavy:** Normal during taper. This does NOT predict race day. Run the 3km, do the strides, go home. Taper legs lie.
- **Do NOT exceed 4km total.** No "feeling good" bonus distance. The race is in 3 days.
- **No strength. No gym. No "just a few sets."** Yesterday was the final strength session. The next time you pick up a weight is after Sunday's race.
