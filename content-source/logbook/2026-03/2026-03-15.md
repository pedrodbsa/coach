### Gel Fueling Protocol (First Time)

**This is a rehearsal for race day.** The goal is to test your gut's tolerance, practice the mechanics of eating while running, and figure out what works before March 29.

**Gel inventory (shared with brother):**

| Gel | Carbs | Extras | Assigned to |
| --- | ----- | ------ | ----------- |
| Caffeine gel #1 | 25g | 106mg caffeine | **Pedro (km 8)** |
| 40g carb gel | 40g | — | **Pedro (km 15)** |
| Caffeine gel #2 | 25g | 106mg caffeine | Brother |
| Sodium gel | 20g | 50mg sodium | Brother |

**Timing plan:**

| When | What | Why |
| ---- | ---- | --- |
| **Km 8 (~55-60 min)** | Caffeine gel (106mg, 25g carb) + water | Caffeine first — takes 15-20 min to kick in, boost for second half. |
| **Km 15 (~100-105 min)** | 40g carb gel + water (20km only) | Pure energy top-up for the final kms. |

**How to take a gel (seriously, this matters):**
1. **Slow down or walk for 15-20 seconds.** Don't try to take it at full pace your first time.
2. Tear the top, squeeze the whole thing into your mouth in 2-3 gulps.
3. Chase with 100-150ml water (unless isotonic gel).
4. Resume running. You may feel a brief heaviness — normal. Passes in 2-3 min.

**What to watch for:**
- **Normal:** Sweet taste, mild stomach awareness for 2-5 min, energy boost 10-15 min later
- **Manageable:** Light nausea for 1-2 min — slow down, sip water, it passes
- **Abort fueling (don't take gel 2):** Cramping, bloating, urgent GI distress. This means that brand/type doesn't work for you — try a different one next time

**Race day plan (if gels work today):** Gel at 45 min, 75 min, and 100 min. ~70-75g carbs total across the HM. This is the standard protocol for sub-2h efforts.

**If you don't have gels:** 2-3 medjool dates at km 8 work as an alternative test. Similar carb load (~20g per date), natural sugars, easier on some stomachs. But dates don't travel as well on race day — gels are worth testing.
