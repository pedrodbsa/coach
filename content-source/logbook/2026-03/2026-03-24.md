## Plan

### Session: Final Tune-Up — 3x1km at HM Pace (~7km total)

**Timing:** Late morning / lunchtime | **Distance:** ~7 km | **Duration:** ~42-45 min
**Pre-run:** 1 banana + coffee — **mandatory.** This is a quality session, not fasted.

**Pre-run warm-up (Daily 5):**

1. Pogo Hops — 30s
2. A-Skips — 30s
3. Leg Swings (Front/Back) — 30s/side
4. Leg Swings (Lateral) — 30s/side
5. Walking Lunges — 30s

| Segment      | Distance | Pace         | HR Target   | Notes                                     |
| ------------ | -------- | ------------ | ----------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Warm-up      | 2 km     | 6:30-7:00/km | <140 bpm    | Easy jog. Let HR settle before reps.      |
| Rep 1        | 1 km     | 5:15-5:25/km | 155-165 bpm | Smooth, controlled. Find the rhythm.      |
| Jog recovery | 60s      | Walk/jog     | <140 bpm    | Full recovery not needed — short session. |
| Rep 2        | 1 km     | 5:15-5:25/km | 155-168 bpm | Same as rep 1. Lock in.                   |
| Jog recovery | 60s      | Walk/jog     | <140 bpm    |                                           |
| Rep 3        | 1 km     | 5:15-5:25/km | 158-170 bpm | Final rep — confident, not heroic.        |
| Cool-down    | 2 km     | 6:30-7:30/km | <140 bpm    | Easy jog, walk last 500m.                 |

**Execution notes:**

- **This is a confidence builder, not a fitness session.** You are NOT gaining fitness today. You are reminding your legs what race pace feels like. The reps should feel controlled, almost easy. If they do, you're ready.
- **5:15-5:25/km is the target.** That's faster than sub-2:00 pace (5:40) but close to your realistic HM pace based on 1:47:58 prediction (~5:07/km). Running 5:15-5:25 in reps is appropriate — race adrenaline and taper freshness close the gap.
- **Do NOT run faster than 5:10/km.** If rep 1 comes back at 5:05, you went too hard. Rein it in for rep 2. This is not a test.
- **If legs feel flat:** Run the reps at 5:25-5:30 instead. The stimulus is the same. Flat legs during taper are normal and do NOT predict race day.
- **3 reps only.** No "bonus" 4th rep. No extending the cool-down into a tempo. You're done when the 3rd rep is done.
- **Total work at pace: 3km.** That's it. Short, sharp, done.
