- **Talk test:** Full sentences comfortably throughout. If you can't talk, walk.
- **RPE:** 4 max. This should feel like a relaxed Sunday jog, not a workout.
- **Fueling:** If run exceeds 90 min, take water at km 10-12 (fountain or carry a bottle). No gels needed at this intensity.
