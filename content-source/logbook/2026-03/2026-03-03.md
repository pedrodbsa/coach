**Execution cues:**

- **Warm-up:** 2km easy + 3 strides. The strides prime fast-twitch fibers before the main set.
- **Rep 1 (calibration):** Start at 4:45/km. Feel out the legs. Check HR at the 1km mark — if already >170, hold 4:45 for the remainder. If <165, you have room to push to 4:40 on rep 2.
- **Rep 2-3:** Settle into whatever pace puts HR at 168-175 by the end of each rep. These should feel like "controlled hard" — RPE 7-8, not 9.
- **Pacing discipline:** All 3 reps within 5 sec/km of each other. You improved from 36s spread (Feb 20) to 16s (Feb 27). Today's target: <10s spread.
- **Recovery jogs:** 2 min easy shuffle. Longer than your usual 90s. Goal is to start each rep relatively fresh — this is about quality, not accumulating fatigue.
- **Talk test:** During reps, short phrases only. If you can hold a conversation, speed up. If you can only grunt, back off.
- **RPE:** 7-8 during reps. Overall session ~7. This should NOT feel like a race effort. You should finish thinking "I could have done 2 more reps."
- **Cool-down:** Walk/very easy jog for 1.5km. Do NOT speed up. You know the drill.
- **Knee monitoring:** If any sharp pain (distinct from the mild 1-2/5 background) during rep 1, stop immediately. Switch to easy 5km MAF. The 10K is more important than this session.
