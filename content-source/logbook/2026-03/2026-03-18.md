**Warm-up checkpoint (after strides, before rep 1):**
- **GREEN (6 reps):** Legs responsive, strides smooth, HR settles <140 within 60s. Yesterday's heaviness is gone.
- **AMBER (4 reps):** Legs still flat, strides sluggish, or HR slow to recover. Do 4×1km — still a quality stimulus.
- **RED (abort):** Heavy legs like yesterday, HR >145 during warm-up jog. Jog 4km easy and go home. Protect the rest of the taper.

**Execution notes:**
- **This is HM goal pace, not a time trial.** 5:15-5:25/km should feel like "I could hold this for 21km." If it feels like race effort on rep 1, you're too fast.
- **1km reps are short.** You'll barely hit target HR before the rep ends. That's fine — the purpose is neuromuscular sharpening and pace calibration, not cardiovascular load.
- **60s recovery between reps.** Walk 15-20s, then easy shuffle. HR won't fully drop — intentional.
- **Pacing discipline:** Start each rep at 5:25. If you drift to 5:10-5:15, that's OK on reps 5-6. NOT on reps 1-3.
- **Yesterday's cardiac drift warning:** If you see HR >170 on rep 2, the body isn't ready. Drop to 4 reps.
- **RPE target:** 6-7 per rep. Overall session ~7. You should finish thinking "I had 2 more reps in me."

**Why 6×1km instead of 6×2km:** Yesterday showed residual fatigue. 6km of quality (6×1km) is half the volume of Saturday's planned 4×2km (8km). Shorter reps = more recovery between efforts, same pace practice, lower cardiac load. Perfect for a taper sharpener.
