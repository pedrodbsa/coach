**Execution notes:**
- **Day after long run.** You said you felt like you didn't run — great. But respect the recovery: if legs feel heavier than expected at km 2, skip strides and just jog.

- **Do NOT extend.** No "feeling good so I'll do 8km." Race is in 6 days. Every extra km is pointless volume.

### Session 2: Light Upper Push (~30 min)

**Timing:** Afternoon (2+ hours after run) | **Duration:** 30 min max
**RPE ceiling: 6.** This is a pump session, not training. Blood flow, muscle stimulus, nothing more.

Last Upper Push logged: Mar 13 (DB Bench 18-20kg, Arnold 10-12kg, Cable Flyes, Dips, Lat Raises, Ab Wheel, Pallof Press). 10 days ago.

**Reduced volume — 2 working sets per exercise:**

| # | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Weight | Rest | Notes |
| - | -------- | ----------- | ------ | ---- | ----- |
| 1 | Barbell Bench Press | 2 x 10 | ~50-55 kg (moderate) | 2 min | Rotate from DB. Controlled, no grind reps. |
| 2 | Overhead Press | 2 x 10-12 | Light-moderate | 90 sec | Rotate from Arnold. Standing, strict form. |
| 3 | Incline DB Press | 2 x 12 | 14-16 kg | 90 sec | Rotate from Cable Flyes. Light pump. |
| 4 | Dips | 2 x 10 | BW | 60 sec | Easy 10, don't push to failure. |
| 5 | Lateral Raises | 2 x 15 | 6 kg | 60 sec | Light, controlled. |
| 6 | Pallof Press | 2 x 10/side | Light cable | 60 sec | Anti-rotation. Slow and controlled. |

**Total: 12 working sets + 2 core sets = 14 sets.** ~30 min. That's 40% less volume than a normal push session.

**Rules:**
- **If any set hits RPE 7, stop that exercise.** Drop the weight or move on.
- **No failure.** Leave 3-4 reps in the tank on every set.
- **No core-intensive exercises that load the trunk** (no ab wheel — save the core for running). Pallof Press is light enough.
- **Hydrate well.** Post-session protein shake as usual.
