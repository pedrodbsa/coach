**Some of the remainder is execution, not fitness.** Jul 13's main was sloppy — a 5:51 km next to a 6:40 km, a **49 s/km spread**. Today's was flat. A steadier hold at the same HR produces a faster average on its own.

**Read: directionally plausible, magnitude untrustworthy. Do not bank an 18 s/km gain.** The series needs a third datapoint taken properly.

**Protocol fix for the next control run — this is the fixable part:** start it in **treadmill mode** (not Run), and **write down the treadmill console's speed setting in km/h**.
