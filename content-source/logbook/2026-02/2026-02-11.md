Race predictions declined 9 seconds this week (5K: 22:11 → 22:20). This is not random noise — it reflects a pattern of running "easy" runs too hard:

| Date   | Run Type  | Avg HR  | MAF Ceiling | Compliant? |

| Feb 9  | Base 5.3km| 144 bpm | 142         | **No** — slightly over |
| Feb 10 | Base 5km  | 144 bpm | 142         | **No** — 176 max spike |

**Diagnosis:** Chronic gray-zone running is accumulating fatigue without building aerobic base. The Garmin algorithm sees declining efficiency (same pace at higher HR) and drops predictions.

**Fix:** Genuinely easy running. Accept 6:30-7:15/km. Walk if HR hits 142. The aerobic adaptations come from volume at low intensity, not from pushing harder. A tempo run right now would add stress to an already fatigued system.

We have 11 days of base phase left (until Feb 22). Make them count by running **truly easy**.
