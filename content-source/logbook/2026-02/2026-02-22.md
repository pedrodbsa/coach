| Segment   | Duration   | Pace         | HR Target   | Notes                               |

**Execution cues:**
- **Replicate last Sunday's effort.** 141 avg HR over 13km was perfect. Add 1km to that.
- Walk every uphill. No exceptions.
- Set Garmin HR alert at 142 bpm. Walk when it buzzes.
- Target 6:45-7:15/km pace. Slower on hills is fine.
- Talk test: full sentences comfortably throughout.
- RPE 4 max.
- **Check in at km 10:** if avg HR is >143, slow down aggressively for the final 4km.
- Cardiac drift is expected over 95+ min — allow up to 145 avg at finish, but aim for 142 or under.
- This is the last easy-only long run. Next week's long run may include HM-pace segments.

**Post-run:** 10-min foam rolling (quads, IT band, calves, glutes).
