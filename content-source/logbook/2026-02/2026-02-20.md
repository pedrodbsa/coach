## Coaching Note: Second Quality Session — Cruise Intervals

This is the other half of the quality reintroduction. Where Tuesday's tempo was continuous sustained effort, cruise intervals are broken up: 5 hard km with 90s jog between. The breaks allow more total threshold volume with less fatigue.

**Key difference from tempo:** Cruise intervals sit at sub-threshold — slightly below the point where you'd start to struggle. The recovery jogs prevent full lactate clearance, teaching your body to run on lactate. Think "controlled" not "comfortable."

**PT legs was yesterday.** If DOMS is significant, drop to 4×1km instead of 5. Don't compromise form.

### Workout: Cruise Intervals

**Timing:** Before lunch | **Target distance:** ~9 km | **Duration:** ~50-55 min
**Pre-run (mandatory):** 1 banana + coffee, 30 min before

| Segment    | Distance/Reps  | Pace           | HR Target   | Notes                               |
| ---------- | -------------- | -------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Warm-up    | 2.0 km         | 6:30-7:00/km   | <135 bpm    | Daily 5 neuromuscular routine first |
| Intervals  | 5 × 1.0 km     | 5:15-5:25/km   | 155-165 bpm | 90 sec jog recovery between reps    |
| Cool-down  | 1.5 km         | 7:00+/km       | <130 bpm    | Walk if needed                      |

**Execution cues:**
- **Warm-up:** 2km genuinely easy. Don't skip this — your legs need it after PT yesterday.
- **First interval:** Start at 5:05/km. Assess how the legs feel. If DOMS is bad, stay at 5:05-5:10 for all reps.
- **HR governor:** Target 155-165 bpm during intervals. If HR hits 170+, you're going too hard — back off pace.
- **Recovery jogs:** 90 seconds at a shuffle/walk. HR should drop to ~130-140. Don't rush these.
- **Pacing discipline:** All 5 reps should be within 5 seconds of each other. Don't start fast and fade — that's gray-zone running by rep 4.
- **Breathing:** Controlled discomfort. You can speak in short phrases. If you're gasping, slow down.
- **Bail-out rule:** If you can't hold sub-5:30/km on rep 3 while staying under HR 170, drop to 4 reps and cool down. This is a reintroduction week.
- **RPE:** 7-8 during intervals, 3-4 during recovery jogs. Overall session RPE should be ~7.

**Recovery jog between intervals:**
- 90 sec easy jog/walk
- Don't stand still — keep moving
- HR should start coming down by 30 sec into the jog
