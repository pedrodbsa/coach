## Coaching Note: First Quality Session in 3 Weeks

This is the most important run of the week. You haven't done any intensity work since late January. The aerobic base is solid (141 avg HR on Sunday's 13km proves it). Now we layer in threshold stimulus.

**Expectations:**
- Tempo pace may feel harder than expected — your legs have been cruising at 6:30-7:15/km for weeks. 5:05-5:15/km will feel like a gear change.
- At this pace, your HR will likely sit around 165-175. That's normal for your physiology — post-smoking cardiovascular profile means higher HR at a given pace. The 3 weeks of MAF work may have improved this, but don't expect magic. We'll see what the data says.
- **RPE is the governor, not HR.** Target RPE 7. If it hits 8+, slow down regardless of pace or HR.
- This is a reintroduction, not a test. The goal is to complete the session feeling "I could have done more."

**Execution cues:**
- **Warm-up is essential.** 2km at genuine easy pace. Don't rush into the tempo.
- **First km of tempo:** Start at 5:15/km. Let the pace come to you. Do NOT charge out at 5:00.
- **RPE governor:** This should feel like hard work but controlled. Short phrases OK. If you can only manage single words, slow down.
- **HR will be high — that's expected.** Don't panic at 170+. Your physiology runs hot. The key metric is RPE and breathing, not the number on your wrist.
- **Ceiling:** If HR hits 178+ or RPE hits 8+, pull back to 5:20/km. No heroics on the reintroduction.
- **Talk test:** You should NOT be able to hold full conversation, but should manage "I'm... doing... fine" without gasping.
- **Last km of tempo:** If feeling good AND RPE is still 7, can push to 5:05/km. If RPE is already 7.5, hold steady.
