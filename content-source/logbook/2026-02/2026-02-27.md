**If outdoor:** Pick a flat, loopable route or a track. Avoid hills during intervals — they'll spike HR unpredictably. A park loop or riverside path works.

**If treadmill:** WU at 8.5 km/h, intervals at 12.0-12.5 km/h, recovery at 7.5 km/h, CD at 8.0 km/h.

**Strides how-to:** On flat ground, smoothly accelerate over 5 sec to ~85-90% sprint, hold 10-15 sec, decelerate. Walk back to start. Controlled and relaxed — high cadence, light feet, tall posture. Not a race.

**Execution cues:**

- **Warm-up:** 2km easy + 3 strides. The strides prime the neuromuscular system for the fast work ahead.
- **Rep 1:** Start at 5:00/km. See where HR goes. This is a calibration rep.
- **Rep 2-3:** If HR was under 175 on rep 1, push to 4:50/km. If it was 175+, hold 5:00.
- **Rep 4-7:** Settle at whichever pace puts you at 175-182 bpm by the end of each 2-min rep. HR will climb across the set — that's expected.
- **Recovery jogs:** 1 min easy shuffle. HR won't fully drop — that's the point. You should start each rep at ~145-155 bpm. Keep moving, don't stand still.
- **The "can I talk?" test:** During intervals, you should only be able to manage single words. If you can speak a full phrase, speed up. If you can't even grunt, ease off.
- **RPE:** 8-9 during work. This is hard. That's the point. Overall session RPE should be ~8.
- **Pacing (outdoor):** Don't blast rep 1 at 4:30 and fade to 5:15 by rep 5. Start conservative, build into it. Check pace after each rep. All reps within 10 sec/km of each other.
- **Bail-out:** If HR exceeds 188 or you feel dizzy/nauseous, stop the set immediately. If you can't maintain pace for the full 2 min on rep 4+, drop to 90-sec reps for the remainder.
- **Cool-down is real:** Walk/very easy jog. Do NOT repeat the Feb 20 cool-down sprint at 5:40/km and HR 166.
