# SUPPLEMENT PROTOCOL - MASTER

> Core stack is deliberately conservative. All listed items are compatible with finasteride 1 mg/day.

## Daily Schedule

> Timing updated 2026-05-31 off the retired intermittent-fasting window — breakfast is now eaten 07:00-09:00 every day, so "first meal" supplements move to breakfast.

### Breakfast (07:00-09:00)

- Vitamin D3 + K2: 4,000-6,000 IU D3 / 100-120 mcg K2
- Multivitamin: 1 tablet

### Creatine Monohydrate — 5 g, one dose/day

Timing is irrelevant for creatine; take the single 5 g dose with whichever shake is convenient — the post-session shake on training days, the breakfast shake on rest days. This is the **same** 5 g already written into the `meal-rotation.md` shakes, not an additional dose.

### Lunch (~13:00)

- Omega-3 Fish Oil: 2 g combined EPA/DHA
- Zinc: 20 mg
- Finasteride: 1 mg

### Pre-Bed (~22:00)

- Magnesium glycinate: 200-350 mg elemental (kept ≤ the 350 mg supplemental UL; the daily food target already supplies ~400 mg). Well-tolerated and corrects a common dietary shortfall; sleep-onset benefit is modest/uncertain — not a primary sleep lever.

## Strategic / Optional

- Caffeine: 200-250 mg before quality sessions or races. **Hard cutoff ~10:00-11:00 on normal days** — tightened from 13:00 because sleep-onset latency is the primary recovery constraint and caffeine's ~5-6 h half-life leaves a meaningful fraction circulating at a 22:00-23:00 bedtime. If a quality session is in the afternoon, that day's caffeine is the pre-session dose — no separate morning cup on top.
- Collagen Peptides: 15 g + ~50 mg vitamin C 45-60 min before running during high-volume blocks or if tendon stiffness appears
- Astaxanthin: not recommended as a daily supplement during aerobic-development blocks; only consider it temporarily for a specific non-performance reason

## As-Needed Only

- Curcumin 1,000 mg with piperine only for pain/inflammation >48 h. Do not use daily during training blocks.

## Compatibility Notes

- Creatine: safe with finasteride
- Vitamin D3 + K2: safe
- Omega-3: safe
- Magnesium: safe
- Zinc: safe if total daily intake stays below the tolerable upper limit
- Curcumin: use sparingly; not a direct finasteride problem, but chronic anti-inflammatory use can blunt adaptation
